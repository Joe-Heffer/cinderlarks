About

The band was formed in 2025 by singer-songwriter Joe Chetcuti. Joe wanted to bring greater depth and texture to the sound of his songs through a full band, leaning on a love of everything from Bowie, Costello, Nick Drake, The Cure and Radiohead to This is the Kit, White Denim and Smog.

The band formed around a nucleus of exceptional individual musicians with broad and eclectic musical tastes.

  • Joe Chetcuti

    Vocals & rhythm guitar

    Nick Drake, Jeff Buckley, Kate Bush aficionado.

  • Rich Mooney

    Lead guitar

    Influenced by the intricate and melodic guitar work of Johnny Marr and the rock anthems of XXXX.

  • Joe Heffer

    Bass

    Channelling the tight rhythmic lock, melody and funk influences of John Paul Jones.

  • Grzegorz Panczyk
